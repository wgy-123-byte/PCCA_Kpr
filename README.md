Propionate concentration in liver is hypothesized to be associated with increased type 2 diabetes (T2D) risk by fueling glucose production. Multi-omics profiling uncovered lysine propionylation (Kpr) at lysine 39 of propionyl-CoA carboxylase alpha (PCCA K39pr) as a central regulatory layer on hepatic gluconeogenesis. Mechanistically, PCCA K39pr, catalyzed by p300 and removed by SIRT1, stabilizes PCCA by attenuating ubiquitin-mediated degradation, thereby boosting hepatic glucose production. Crucially, elevated propionate exacerbated hyperglycemia and hepatic steatosis in db/db mice in a PCCA-Kpr-dependent manner.
