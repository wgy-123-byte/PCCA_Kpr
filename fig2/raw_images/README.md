# Uncropped originals for FIG2

The following files are named in `../F2_image_panels_manifest.csv` and **must be placed in this folder** before the repository is made public. Only the manifest was supplied with the data drop; the image files themselves are not in this archive.

- `F2C.tif` — Panel C: Kpr levels of the Flag-PCCA/ALDH2/PCCB constructs (Uncropped blot)
- `F2D.tif` — Panel D: PCCA Kpr in liver tissue of db/m and db/db mice  (Uncropped blots)
- `F2E.tif` — Panel E: PCCA Kpr level in liver tissue of db/db mice with or without NaP  (Uncropped blots)
- `F2F.tif` — Panel F: PCCA protein level in liver tissues of db/m and db/db mice (Uncropped blot)
- `F2G.tif` — Panel G: PCCA protein level in BH cells after CHX alone or combined with NaP (5 mM) (Uncropped blot)
- `F2H.tif` — Panel H: The binding of HA-Ub to PCCA (Uncropped blot)
- `F2I.tif` — Panel I: PCCA Kpr level after knocking down indicated acetyltransferases in HEK293T cells (Uncropped blot)
- `F2J.tif` — Panel J: PCCA Kpr level after knocking down corresponding sirtuins (SIRTs) in HEK293T cells (Uncropped blots)
- `F2K.tif` — Panel K: The binding of HA-Ub to PCCA after knocking down p300 (Uncropped blot)
- `F2L.tif` — Panel L: PCCA protein level in p300-depleted BH cells after CHX treatment (Uncropped blot)
- `F2M.tif` — Panel M: The binding of HA-Ub to PCCA after knocking down SIRT1 (Uncropped blot)
- `F2N.tif` — Panel N: PCCA protein level in SIRT1-overexpressed BH cells after CHX treatment (Uncropped blot)
- `F2Q.ai` — Panel Q: PCCA (Bos taurus) domain / lysine site map (Editable source)
- `F2R.tif` — Panel R: PCCA Kpr level of Flag-PCCA wild-type (WT) or the indicated lysine mutant plasmid (Uncropped blot)
- `F2S.tif` — Panel S: The binding of HA-Ub to Flag-PCCA WT or the Flag-PCCA K39R (Uncropped blot)
- `F2T.tif` — Panel T: PCCA protein level of Flag-PCCA WT or the Flag-PCCA K39R in BH cells after CHX treatment (Uncropped blot)

Requirements: full uncropped blots with molecular-weight markers, original microscope exports with scale bars, and editable `.ai`/`.svg`/`.pdf` sources for schematic panels. Do not upload cropped or contrast-adjusted derivatives only.
